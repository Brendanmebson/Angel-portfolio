# Angel Mebuge

Angel Mebuge is a digital health and data analytics professional with a strong interest in child health, health equity, and the responsible use of technology in healthcare.

Her work sits at the intersection of public health, data analysis, and digital innovation. She is particularly motivated by questions around access to care, how evidence can inform decisions in health systems, and how thoughtful use of data and technology can improve outcomes for vulnerable communities.

## About Angel

- Background in anatomy and public health
- Focus on digital health, health research, and healthcare innovation
- Interest in child health, maternal and neonatal care, and health equity
- Experience with data analysis, Excel, Power BI, and research-driven problem solving
- Strong interest in using evidence to guide health programmes and service delivery

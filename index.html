Newborn jaundice on darker skin

Digital health concept · Child health

Jaundice is harder to see on darker skin, so it is often caught late. This phone-based concept guides caregivers and health workers through photographing a newborn against a white reference, gives a risk level, and points them to care in time. It supports care-seeking. It does not diagnose.

Role
Project lead and creator
Stage

For

Where

HopeWell outreach review

Programme analytics · Community health

About me

Anatomy first, then public health, and always one question: are health systems reaching children?

My work sits where data, research and digital tools meet. I analyse health programme data, make it readable for the people who decide budgets, and design tools that fit the realities of the families and health workers who will use them.

I care about responsible technology in healthcare: tools that support clinical judgement rather than replace it, built with the context, the data and the people they are meant to serve.

Data should show not only what is happening in health, but who it is happening to.

What I bring

Data analytics

Excel, Power BI, data cleaning, analysis and visualisation applied to health and programme data.

Digital health and research

Health research, product concepts for child health, responsible AI and literature review.

Leadership

Leading analysis teams, coordinating projects and presenting findings to decision-makers.

What drives the work

Child health
Access to care, outcomes and the gaps that hit the youngest patients hardest.
Health equity
Why some groups keep receiving worse care, and what data, policy and design can do about it.
Digital health
Tools that strengthen access and delivery without losing sight of the community using them.
Data analytics
Analysis and charts clear enough that a programme manager can act on them the same week.
Health research
Reading the evidence carefully and turning findings into something practical.
Responsible AI
Whose data, whose context, whose interests: the questions to ask before an algorithm reaches a clinic.

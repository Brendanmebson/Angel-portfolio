About Me

Where curiosity meets commitment

I have a background in Anatomy and Public Health, with interests spanning data analytics, digital health research, and healthcare innovation. My interest in child health has shaped much of the work I pursue — particularly questions around access to care, health equity, and how technology can be applied thoughtfully to real healthcare challenges.

My Approach

People before platforms, always

I’m interested in using data and research to understand health problems, communicate insights, and explore practical digital solutions. My work includes health data analysis, data visualization, and digital health projects focused on child health and improving access to appropriate care.

I’m particularly interested in the responsible use of technology in healthcare — where digital tools complement human expertise and respond to the realities of the people and communities they are designed to serve.

Areas of Interest

The threads that run through everything

  • Child HealthUnderstanding access to care, health equity, and outcomes for children — with a focus on how research and data can surface the gaps that affect those most in need.
  • Digital HealthExploring how digital tools can be applied thoughtfully to real healthcare challenges, strengthening access and delivery without losing sight of the communities they are designed to serve.
  • Data AnalyticsUsing data to understand health problems and communicate insights — through analysis, visualisation, and the kind of clarity that helps people make better decisions.
  • Health ResearchEvidence-based approaches to understanding healthcare challenges, reviewing literature, and translating findings into insights that are practical and meaningful.
  • Responsible AIAsking the critical questions about algorithmic tools in health settings: whose data, whose context, and whose interests? Technology should serve people, not the other way around.
  • Health EquityInterrogating why some populations consistently receive worse care — and what data, policy, and digital innovation can do to close those gaps.

About Me

Where curiosity meets commitment

I have a background in Anatomy and Public Health, with interests spanning data analytics, digital health research, and healthcare innovation. My work is shaped by a genuine interest in child health, health equity, and how technology can be used responsibly to improve lives.

My Approach

People before platforms, always

I believe technology in healthcare must start with the people it is meant to serve. Every data question I ask begins not with what the data can show, but with what it means for a patient, a family, or a community.

My background in Anatomy and Public Health gives me both a clinical lens and a population perspective. I try to hold both at once — understanding the individual and the system simultaneously.

Areas of Interest

Six threads, one purpose

  • Child Health & Digital HealthResearching access to care and exploring how digital tools can make a meaningful difference for children, families, and underserved communities.
  • Data Analytics & Health ResearchUsing data to understand health challenges, visualise patterns and communicate insights that drive better decisions and more equitable outcomes.
  • Responsible AI & Health EquityEnsuring that technology in healthcare is ethical, inclusive, and truly responsive to the communities it is designed to serve — not just those easiest to reach.
  • Public Health & InnovationBridging the gap between evidence-based research and practical digital solutions that work in real-world health contexts, particularly in Africa.
